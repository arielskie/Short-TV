# 💵 Advertising & Monetization Settings

Monetize non-paying viewers with interstitial ads, banner slots, rewarded video ads for free coins, and third-party ad networks.

---

## 📍 Where to Find
Go to **WordPress Admin → ShortTV Hub → Settings → Advertising & Monetization** tab.

---

## 🛠️ Monetization Modules

| Module | Description | Configuration Options |
|---|---|---|
| **Rewarded Video Ads (Watch to Earn Coins)** | Allows free viewers to watch a short sponsored video in exchange for coins (e.g. +50 coins per ad). | Reward Amount (`50`), Daily Max Ads (`5`), Ad Network ID. |
| **Episode Interstitial Ads** | Pops up a clean video or display ad between vertical episode swipes for non-VIP members. | Frequency (e.g. `Every 3 Episodes`), Skip delay (`5s`). |
| **Banner Ad Slots** | Displays responsive responsive banner units on Homepage, Drama Info Drawer, and Search feed. | Google AdSense, Adsterra, PropellerAds, or Custom HTML/JS script. |
| **VIP Auto Ad-Bypass** | Automatically removes all ads for active VIP Subscribers. | `Always Enabled` (Provides incentive to purchase VIP). |

---

## 💡 Supported Ad Networks
* **Google AdSense**
* **Adsterra / PropellerAds**
* **Custom VAST / VPAID Video Ad Tags**
* **Direct Custom HTML / JavaScript Ad Codes**
