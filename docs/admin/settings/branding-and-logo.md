# 🎨 Branding & Logo Settings

This guide explains how to customize the brand identity, logo assets, dimensions, and display modes across ShortTV.

---

## 📍 Where to Find
Go to **WordPress Admin → ShortTV Hub → Settings → Branding & Logo** tab.

---

## 🛠️ Configuration Options

| Option | Description | Recommended Setting |
|---|---|---|
| **Custom Website & App Logo** | Upload your brand PNG, SVG, or JPG logo. Renders across desktop navbar, mobile header, splash screen, and favicon. | Transparent SVG or 2x PNG (e.g. `240x60px`). |
| **Logo Display Mode** | Choose between `Image Only` (shows logo graphic only) or `Image Icon + Text` (shows icon plus brand text). | `Image Only` (modern streaming app look). |
| **Brand Display Name** | The text name used in title tags, SEO meta, notification messages, and in `Image Icon + Text` mode. | e.g. `ShortTV` or `DramaStream`. |
| **Desktop Logo Dimensions** | Custom height/width constraints for desktop headers. | Height: `42px` – `50px`, Width: `Auto`. |
| **Mobile Logo Dimensions** | Height/width constraints for mobile top bars. | Height: `32px` – `36px`, Width: `Auto`. |

---

## 💡 Best Practices
* **Use Transparent Backgrounds:** Always export your logo as a transparent SVG or 32-bit PNG to ensure it looks sharp on both dark cinematic backgrounds and light modal surfaces.
* **Auto Dimensions:** Keep Width as `Auto` so your logo maintains its natural aspect ratio when adjusting the height.
